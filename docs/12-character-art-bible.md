# 12 — Character & Illustration Art Bible

**Status:** Approved character direction; final production artwork remains to be created  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Updated:** 2026-11-07

## 1. Purpose

Characters make RIOTWORKS recognizable and give specialist operations an expressive identity. They are original members of the MoonWitness creative universe. They are not literal depictions of sacred or historical religious figures.

## 2. Core cast

### THE SCOUT
Role: discovery, evidence, and intelligence.
Personality: curious, fast, analytical, observant.
Silhouette: agile explorer with signal-finding equipment and practical streetwear.
Visual motifs: signal rings, maps, evidence tags, scanner shapes.
Usage: Signal Room, opportunity discovery, trend scan, research empty states.

### THE MAKER
Role: creation and production.
Personality: inventive, expressive, experimental, slightly chaotic.
Silhouette: workshop creator with tools and distinctive creative accessories.
Visual motifs: modular parts, ink marks, sketch lines, production sparks.
Usage: The Forge, product creation, asset variations, production states.

### ZILL
Role: integrity, rights, quality, and protection.
Personality: silent, precise, disciplined, protective.
Silhouette: unmistakable guardian with a geometric protective form; avoid copying existing superhero armor or masks.
Visual motifs: shield geometry, inspection marks, boundary lines, verified stamps.
Usage: ZILL Watch, policy warnings, provenance, quality reports.

### THE KEEPER
Role: assets, versioning, archives, and portfolio continuity.
Personality: meticulous, strategic, patient, reliable.
Silhouette: archivist/engineer hybrid with cataloging instruments.
Visual motifs: index tabs, archive marks, version symbols, catalog frames.
Usage: Sidra Archive, version history, product lifecycle, Aftershock.

## 3. Character consistency

For every character create:
- Character one-sheet.
- Front, side, and three-quarter views.
- Silhouette sheet.
- Expression sheet.
- Pose/action sheet.
- Color and monochrome versions.
- Dark/light background tests.
- SVG/vector master or high-quality source file.
- Usage and exclusion notes.

Keep consistent proportions, key clothing/accessory details, palette, and visual motifs.

## 4. Art style

- Original comic illustration.
- Strong ink contour.
- Controlled halftone.
- Screen-print color blocks.
- Expressive but purposeful gestures.
- Punk/editorial graphic composition.
- Distinct silhouette at small avatar sizes.
- Readable on both dark and paper backgrounds.

Avoid generic AI character drift. Use approved canonical source artwork and edit it deliberately.

## 5. Respect and IP rules

- No copied comic, manga, game, film, or mascot characters.
- No imitation of a living artist's exact style.
- No use of unlicensed logos, celebrity likenesses, or protected designs.
- No word ALLAH on character clothing.
- Do not trivialize sacred language, figures, or religious symbolism.
- Do not imply divine endorsement or spiritual authority.
- Characters are product-fiction roles and must never be presented as real people or religious entities.

## 6. Use in the product

Use characters as:
- Agent identity portraits.
- Onboarding guides.
- Contextual illustrations.
- Empty-state storytelling.
- Production workflow markers.
- Inspection and warning explainers.
- Campaign artwork.

Do not put a character in every card. Use them as recognizable anchors, not UI clutter.

## 7. Asset production and approval

- Maintain source files and export versions.
- Keep a single canonical master per character.
- Record author, generation/edit tool, license, and revisions.
- Review visual consistency at multiple sizes.
- Get owner approval before adopting a new canonical design.
