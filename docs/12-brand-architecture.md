# MOONWITNESS Brand Architecture

**Version:** 1.0
**Decision:** MOONWITNESS is the master brand.

## Brand hierarchy

| Layer | Name | Role | Identity rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Master | MOONWITNESS | Parent identity and product promise | Owns master symbol, wordmark, voice, and core palette |
| Experience | Observatory | Research, observation, evidence workflows | Uses master identity with instrument UI patterns |
| Experience | Archive | Records, memory, retrieval, provenance | Uses master identity with editorial/archive patterns |
| Program | MOONWITNESS XV | Program/release designation | Typographic descriptor only; never a competing logo |
| Studio | RIOTWORKS | Independent creative/engineering studio | Separate identity; attribution only where relevant |

## Brand promise

A bridge between human curiosity, evidence, and a clearer view of the world.

## Core line

SEE MORE. KNOW MORE.

## Verbal system

Primary verbs: OBSERVE · RECORD · VERIFY · CONNECT · UNDERSTAND.

Use confident but measured language. Distinguish facts, interpretations, hypotheses, and unknowns. The brand must never imply certainty that the evidence does not support.

## Identity lockups

- Primary: symbol + MOONWITNESS wordmark.
- Compact: symbol only.
- Horizontal: symbol left, wordmark right.
- Descriptor: master lockup plus a small product descriptor (e.g. OBSERVATORY).
- Monochrome: one-color symbol/wordmark for stamps, print, and low-color contexts.

Do not append RIOTWORKS to the product lockup by default. Do not create independent marks for every feature or module.

## Asset governance

- Source files live in `packages/assets/assets`; declarations live in `packages/assets/src/registry.ts`.
- Each asset records source, license, usage status, accessibility, dimensions, theme, and tags.
- Current original assets are internal project work and remain UNLICENSED for external redistribution until the repository owner declares rights.
- Font families are recommendations only; no font binaries are bundled.
- References to XIV/XV are research inputs, not licenses to copy their artwork.
