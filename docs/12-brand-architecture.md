# MOONWITNESS Brand Architecture

**Version:** 1.3  
**Decision:** MOONWITNESS is the master brand.

## Brand hierarchy
| Layer | Name | Role | Identity rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Master | MOONWITNESS | Parent identity and product promise | Owns master symbol, wordmark, voice, and core palette |
| Experience | Observatory | Research, observation, evidence workflows | Master identity + instrument UI |
| Experience | Archive | Records, memory, retrieval, provenance | Master identity + editorial/archive UI |
| Program | MOONWITNESS XV | Program/release designation | Typographic descriptor only |
| Studio | RIOTWORKS | Independent creative/engineering studio | Separate identity; attribution only where relevant |

## Message architecture
**Core Promise:** SEE MORE. KNOW MORE.  
**Brand Manifesto:** WHEN THE MOON RISES, WE SHARE MESSAGES.  
**Shorthand:** WMR-WSM.  
**Operational verbs:** OBSERVE · RECORD · VERIFY · CONNECT · UNDERSTAND · SHARE.

## Identity lockups
- Primary: lunar symbol + original outlined MOONWITNESS wordmark + Core Promise.
- Compact: symbol or MW monogram.
- Horizontal: symbol left, wordmark right.
- Descriptor: master lockup plus a small product descriptor.
- Monochrome: one-color artwork for stamps and low-color contexts.
- Manifesto: separate editorial/campaign line, not forced into compact logo.

Do not append RIOTWORKS to product lockups. Do not create independent marks for every module.

## Asset and typography governance
- Source files live in `packages/assets/assets`; declarations live in `packages/assets/src/registry.ts`.
- Original SVG assets and the custom font remain UNLICENSED for external redistribution until the repository owner declares rights.
- MOONWITNESS Signal Display 0.1 is a locally bundled prototype. Signal Text and Instrument use system stacks; full glyph coverage remains a future milestone.
