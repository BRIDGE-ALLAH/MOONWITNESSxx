# MOONWITNESS Design DNA

**Status:** Approved direction — brand architecture v1.0  
**Updated:** 2026-11-08

## Creative thesis
MOONWITNESS is an evidence-first observatory and a human knowledge-sharing movement: observe the world, record what matters, verify what can be known, connect discoveries, and share meaning with others.

**Art direction:** MOONPUNK EDITORIAL + CINEMATIC OBSERVATORY + EVIDENCE ARCHIVE + INSTRUMENT BRUTALISM.

The identity combines lunar symbolism, archival credibility, cinematic observatory atmosphere, precise research instruments, and human warmth. It must feel curious, intelligent, accountable, humane, and quietly unconventional—not like a generic AI product, crypto project, game HUD, or sci-fi dashboard.

## Messaging
- **Core Promise:** SEE MORE. KNOW MORE.
- **Brand Manifesto:** WHEN THE MOON RISES, WE SHARE MESSAGES. (WMR-WSM)
- **Operational verbs:** OBSERVE · RECORD · VERIFY · CONNECT · UNDERSTAND · SHARE

## Brand architecture
- **MOONWITNESS** — master brand.
- **Observatory** — research, observation, and evidence experience.
- **Archive** — durable knowledge, records, provenance, and retrieval.
- **MOONWITNESS XV** — program/release expression.
- **RIOTWORKS** — independent creative/engineering studio; not part of product lockups by default.

## Visual principles
Lunar geometry; witness aperture symbolism; evidence editorial hierarchy; instrument precision; warm paper and restrained tactile texture. Avoid surveillance-eye clichés, fake coordinates, and decorative pseudo-science.

## Typography
- **Identity lettering:** original outlined MOONWITNESS Signal wordmark and MW monogram.
- **Signal Display:** original local WOFF2 display face for headings and short uppercase labels.
- **Signal Text:** platform UI sans for body copy and controls.
- **Signal Instrument:** platform monospace for IDs, timestamps, coordinates, and provenance.
- **Long-form reading:** platform serif fallback where appropriate.

The first font cut covers uppercase Latin, digits, common punctuation, and lowercase codepoints mapped to uppercase. Distinct lowercase forms, Indonesian diacritics, extended Latin, kerning, hinting, and cross-platform rendering remain in development. See `packages/assets/fonts/MOONWITNESS-SIGNAL.md`.

## Texture, accessibility, motion
Keep lunar grids, contours, halftone, and archival rules low-opacity and away from dense data. Meaningful artwork requires alt text; decorative assets must be hidden from assistive technology. Respect reduced motion and never encode meaning through color alone. Maintain WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, visible focus, keyboard operation, and readable zoom/reflow.
