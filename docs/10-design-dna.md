# MOONWITNESS Design DNA

**Status:** Canonical visual direction — aligned to the MOONWITNESS master brand  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Updated:** 2026-11-08

## 1. Creative thesis

MOONWITNESS is an evidence-first observatory and a human knowledge-sharing movement. It notices the signal inside the noise, preserves what was witnessed, tests what can be tested, connects what belongs together, and shares discoveries with context.

**Master art direction:**

> **MOONPUNK EDITORIAL × CINEMATIC OBSERVATORY × EVIDENCE ARCHIVE × INSTRUMENT BRUTALISM**

The identity should feel like a meeting point between a midnight observatory, a field notebook, a serious research archive, and an independent editorial publication. It is cinematic without becoming science-fiction theatre; expressive without becoming chaotic; precise without feeling cold; and human without becoming sentimental.

MOONWITNESS is not a generic AI dashboard, crypto interface, surveillance console, game HUD, or template-driven SaaS product.

## 2. The story translated into design

The brand story is the visual system's source of meaning:

- **Moon** means changing perspective, cycles, timing, reflection, and illumination. Use lunar geometry as a quiet structural language—not astrology, occult decoration, or a claim of supernatural power.
- **Witness** means accountable attention. Represent it through an aperture, observation marker, annotation, source trail, or recorded moment—not a surveillance eye or targeting reticle.
- **Signal** means a meaningful observation emerging from noise. Give it hierarchy, context, and a traceable source—not arbitrary neon decoration.
- **Message** means information shared with care. Let source, author, timestamp, confidence, and uncertainty remain close to the information they qualify.
- **Archive** means memory with provenance. Show how a record was made, changed, connected, and retrieved.
- **Humanity** means curiosity, dignity, authorship, and the responsibility to correct mistakes.

The visual language must make the product's epistemic values visible: observation is not proof; a record is not automatically verified; a relationship is not automatically causation; and an unknown is not a failure to hide.

## 3. Brand messaging hierarchy

### Core Promise

**SEE MORE. KNOW MORE.**

The enduring product promise. Use it in the primary brand lockup, product entry points, and concise brand signatures.

### Brand Manifesto

**WHEN THE MOON RISES, WE SHARE MESSAGES.**

The poetic and communal expression of the brand. Use it in campaign/editorial contexts, About pages, launch narratives, community communications, and selected onboarding moments. It is not a replacement for the Core Promise and should not crowd compact UI.

### Operational verbs

**OBSERVE · RECORD · VERIFY · CONNECT · UNDERSTAND · SHARE**

These verbs describe the knowledge practice. They may inspire navigation, section labels, and storytelling, but should not be forced into every screen.

## 4. Brand personality

| Dimension | MOONWITNESS | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Intelligence | Curious, rigorous, observant | Omniscient, arrogant, pseudo-scientific |
| Atmosphere | Nocturnal, cinematic, reflective | Horror, occult, surveillance theatre |
| Structure | Editorial, measured, traceable | Generic SaaS cards everywhere |
| Energy | Quiet confidence with moments of signal | Constant neon, motion, and noise |
| Humanity | Warm, respectful, authored | Stock AI faces and anonymous hype |
| Expression | Funky, independent, purposeful | Random decoration or novelty for novelty's sake |

The interface should be calm when people are reading, comparing, deciding, or operating. Expressive moments should be deliberate and memorable rather than continuous.

## 5. Visual grammar

### 5.1 Lunar and observational geometry

Use crescents, open arcs, orbital paths, lunar phases, horizons, calibrated rings, and small star points as a coherent visual vocabulary.

- An **open arc** can imply a view in progress or an incomplete perspective.
- An **orbital path** may express an evidenced relationship, sequence, or route; never invent a data relationship merely to fill space.
- A **horizon** can create a strong transition in editorial storytelling or page entry.
- A **star point** can mark a selected signal or point of attention; it is not a rating, success state, or evidence verdict.
- A **measured ring** can frame a focal object or represent a real bounded measurement; do not use fake precision.

Never fill operational screens with decorative coordinates, fake telemetry, random constellations, pseudo-scientific labels, or meaningless reticles.

### 5.2 Evidence editorial

Use source labels, dates, index numbers, captions, citations, marginal notes, ruled separators, confidence language, and provenance trails as genuine information architecture.

Metadata should be readable and useful. Do not imitate research or official documents with fabricated seals, invented certifications, fake stamps, or meaningless IDs.

### 5.3 Instrument precision

Use disciplined grids, aligned columns, fine rules, restrained tick marks, stable spacing, and clear information hierarchy. Dense views should still feel measured and navigable, not like a cockpit filled with unnecessary controls.

### 5.4 Human warmth and print character

Use warm paper, tactile grain, restrained halftone, lunar topography, editorial cropping, and original human-centered illustration. Character and people imagery should preserve dignity; anonymous silhouettes, gestures, objects, environments, or back-facing figures are preferred when a recognizable face is not essential.

Textures are a supporting material, not the information itself. Keep them low-opacity and away from dense data, body text, status indicators, focus rings, and form controls.

## 6. Experience skins

The master identity has three initial visual experiences. They share the same logo, semantic meaning, interaction behavior, accessibility contract, and evidence vocabulary. They differ in composition, surface material, expressive energy, and task emphasis.

### 6.1 Observatory — cinematic instrument

**Role:** Default application experience for operational, monitoring, research, decision, and workbench interfaces.

- Dark-first, nocturnal, cinematic, and measured.
- Void canvas, layered Observatory panels, Lunar Silver secondary information, and Signal Gold for purposeful focus.
- Fine orbital geometry, calibrated rules, and instrument framing at restrained opacity.
- Strong hierarchy for live state, evidence, source, timestamp, confidence, and uncertainty.
- Supports compact and comfortable density where the actual workflow requires it.
- Avoid dashboard-card repetition, decorative HUD chrome, and bright effects around every metric.

Use for Headquarter operational workspaces, system status, observation workbenches, review queues, and data-dense interfaces.

### 6.2 Archive — warm evidence editorial

**Role:** Reading, research, documentation, records, knowledge, provenance, and long-form content.

- Light-first, warm paper, deep ink, and restrained editorial contrast.
- Document-first composition, generous reading width, clear title/metadata/content rhythm, citations, index markers, and marginalia.
- Editorial serif may be used selectively for long-form titles or quotations when a licensed family is available.
- Tables and technical details remain legible and structured; paper texture never compromises readability.
- It is a distinct editorial composition, not merely Observatory with inverted colors.

Use for Archive, source records, knowledge articles, research reports, documentation, and evidence histories.

### 6.3 Signal — Moonpunk editorial

**Role:** Public storytelling, entry experiences, onboarding, community, campaign, release narratives, and selected creative-commerce surfaces.

- High-impact editorial typography, strong scale contrast, asymmetrical layouts, hard-edged blocks, and deliberate negative space.
- Restrained grunge print, halftone, manga motion lines, paper cuts, ink marks, and poster-like composition.
- Signal Red, Eclipse Violet, and Signal Gold may become more expressive accents, but status and accessibility colors remain semantic and consistent.
- Use original illustration and art direction. Keep the MOONWITNESS mark and narrative intact.
- Preserve readable copy, clear actions, and truthful status; expressive does not mean confusing.

Use for landing pages, onboarding introductions, community/editorial stories, campaign pages, and selected product launch moments. Do not use it as the default for dense operational tables, finance, security, identity, settings, or lengthy forms.

**Signal is the expressive MOONWITNESS skin—not a ROCKSOUL rebrand and not a RIOTWORKS skin.** Its Moonpunk energy must remain purposeful, respectful, and evidence-aware.

## 7. Color system

### Brand primitives

| Token | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Void | `#090A0F` | Deep nocturnal canvas |
| Observatory | `#121521` | Instrument surface |
| Slate | `#242A38` | Secondary dark surface |
| Moonlight | `#F4F1E8` | Warm light / paper |
| Lunar Silver | `#AEB6C6` | Quiet information and linework |
| Signal Gold | `#E8BD62` | Primary identity emphasis |
| Eclipse Violet | `#7664A8` | Atmospheric secondary accent |
| Signal Red | `#C95B5B` | Exceptional signal / expressive accent |
| Deep Ink | `#171923` | Light-theme text |

Brand primitives are source material, not direct substitutes for semantic component tokens.

### Semantic colors

Define independent semantic roles for background, foreground, panel, muted text, border, focus, selected, disabled, success, warning, danger, information, and neutral. Meaning must not depend on color alone.

- Gold does not mean success.
- Red does not mean decoration when it conveys danger or destructive action.
- Violet does not mean an arbitrary AI gradient.
- Accent colors must meet the contrast requirements of the component they are used on.

Rendered text and controls must meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast requirements. Token calculations are an early check, not a substitute for browser verification.

## 8. Typography

- **Display:** Space Grotesk 600–700; distinctive but controlled editorial headlines.
- **Interface/body:** Space Grotesk 400–600; readable in operational sizes and at zoom.
- **Technical/data:** IBM Plex Mono 400–500 for IDs, timestamps, coordinates, version labels, evidence IDs, and provenance.
- **Long-form editorial:** a licensed serif may be introduced selectively for Archive and Signal storytelling, never as a required dependency for baseline legibility.
- **Fallbacks:** system sans-serif and system monospace must preserve usable layout if remote font loading fails.

Do not rely on all-uppercase microcopy for every label. Use uppercase as an editorial accent or compact technical label, not as a replacement for readable sentence case.

Font binaries must not be bundled or redistributed until pinned source, hashes, license conditions, and owner approval are recorded. The experimental MOONWITNESS Signal font is not approved for runtime use.

## 9. Iconography, marks, and graphic assets

- Preserve one MOONWITNESS master symbol and wordmark across skins.
- Use product descriptors such as OBSERVATORY, ARCHIVE, or SIGNAL as typography—not separate master logos.
- Use one consistent functional icon family with predictable sizing, stroke weight, and alignment.
- Icons must describe an action or concept; do not use lunar symbols as decoration beside every control.
- Original illustrations, textures, and patterns belong in `@riotworks/assets` with source, owner, license status, dimensions, theme applicability, and accessibility metadata.
- A generated image is illustration, not documentary evidence. Evidence media requires source and provenance.

## 10. Composition and layout

- Use deliberate grids, clear alignment, and purposeful asymmetry.
- Give every screen one dominant task and one clear information hierarchy.
- Separate navigation, context, evidence, decision, and action regions.
- Keep provenance and uncertainty adjacent to the claims they qualify.
- Use editorial title + metadata + content composition for reading.
- Use instrument panels and stable tables for live operations.
- Use poster composition for storytelling, not as a universal component layout.
- Avoid a page being made from identical rounded cards without a reason.
- Preserve responsive reflow and reading order; desktop composition must not be the only designed state.

## 11. Surface, shape, and texture

- Prefer restrained radii, measured borders, clear layering, and purposeful surface elevation.
- Observatory may use layered dark surfaces and hairline instrument rules.
- Archive may use paper-like surfaces, editorial separators, and restrained printed details.
- Signal may use square/controlled corners, poster blocks, paper cuts, and high-contrast sections.
- Avoid blur, glow, shadow, grain, and gradients as default decoration.
- Use one or two strong visual motifs in a composition; do not stack every effect at once.

## 12. Motion and interaction

- Motion communicates state, direction, hierarchy, or continuity—not decoration.
- Use short, purposeful transitions; never animate metrics in a way that implies false change.
- Orbital or background motion is optional and off by default for operational screens.
- Avoid auto-playing parallax, flashing, rapid strobing, and motion that competes with reading.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and provide equivalent static communication.
- Keep interaction semantics, focus behavior, and feedback consistent across all skins.

## 13. Accessibility and dignity

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA for text, controls, and interaction.
- Use semantic HTML, useful accessible names, visible focus, keyboard operation, and correct reading order.
- Never encode status through color, texture, position, or animation alone.
- Meaningful artwork needs alt text; decorative SVG and texture must be hidden from assistive technology.
- Do not place texture behind dense text, chart labels, controls, or focus indicators.
- Preserve legibility at 200% zoom and narrow mobile widths.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and the dignity of people represented in imagery.

## 14. Forbidden shortcuts

- Generic blue-purple AI gradients and template-driven SaaS layouts.
- Generic surveillance-eye, all-seeing-eye, targeting-reticle, or occult-symbol clichés.
- Fake data labels, fake coordinates, fake certificates, fake charts, or pseudo-scientific ornament.
- Riotworks Acid Lime / punk-comic styling as the default MOONWITNESS identity.
- Turning every surface into a game HUD or poster.
- Using random stock/AI imagery as evidence or source of truth.
- Unlicensed fonts, copied marks, stock character art, or artwork copied from XIV/XV reference repositories.
- Sacred, religious, or cultural symbols used as decoration.
- A separate logo or unrelated palette for every skin, feature, module, or release.

## 15. Recognition test

Hide the logo and ask whether the screen still feels like MOONWITNESS through its lunar geometry, evidence hierarchy, typography, warm/cool material language, restrained instrument precision, and human editorial character.

Then ask:

1. Is the main task immediately clear?
2. Can a reader identify what is observed, recorded, verified, uncertain, or interpreted?
3. Is source/provenance close to the information it qualifies?
4. Does the selected skin serve the task rather than decorate it?
5. Does the screen remain accessible, responsive, and truthful without its visual effects?

If not, the screen is not ready even if it looks distinctive.
