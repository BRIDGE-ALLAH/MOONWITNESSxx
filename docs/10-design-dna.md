# MOONWITNESS Design DNA

**Status:** Approved direction — brand architecture v1.0  
**Updated:** 2026-11-08

## Creative thesis
MOONWITNESS is an evidence-first observatory and a human knowledge-sharing movement: observe the world, record what matters, verify what can be known, connect discoveries, and share meaning with others.

**Art direction:** MOONPUNK EDITORIAL + CINEMATIC OBSERVATORY + EVIDENCE ARCHIVE + INSTRUMENT BRUTALISM.

The identity combines lunar symbolism, archival credibility, cinematic observatory atmosphere, precise research instruments, and human warmth. It must feel curious, intelligent, accountable, humane, and quietly unconventional—not like a generic AI product, crypto project, game HUD, or sci-fi dashboard.

## Messaging
- **Core Promise:** SEE MORE. KNOW MORE.
- **Brand Manifesto:** WHEN THE MOON RISES, WE SHARE MESSAGES. (WMR-WSM)
- **Operational verbs:** OBSERVE · RECORD · VERIFY · CONNECT · UNDERSTAND · SHARE

The Core Promise explains what the product enables. The Manifesto explains why discoveries are shared.

## Brand architecture
- **MOONWITNESS** — master brand and public-facing identity.
- **Observatory** — research, observation, and evidence experience.
- **Archive** — durable knowledge, records, provenance, and retrieval.
- **MOONWITNESS XV** — program/release expression.
- **RIOTWORKS** — independent creative/engineering studio; not part of product lockups by default.

## Visual principles
1. Lunar geometry: crescents, orbital paths, phases, horizons, star points, calibrated rings.
2. Witness symbolism: aperture, record marker, or star; never a literal surveillance eye.
3. Evidence editorial: index numbers, captions, source labels, dates, citations, marginal notes, and ruled paper.
4. Instrument precision: fine lines, measured grids, coordinates, restrained ticks, clear hierarchy.
5. Human warmth: warm paper, tactile grain, imperfect print details, original human-centered illustration.

## Typography
- **Identity lettering:** original outlined MOONWITNESS Signal wordmark and MW monogram.
- **Signal Display:** in-house display direction for headlines; condensed system fallback until the complete original font is compiled.
- **Signal Text:** platform UI sans for body copy and controls.
- **Signal Instrument:** platform monospace for IDs, timestamps, coordinates, evidence IDs, and provenance.
- **Long-form reading:** platform serif fallback where appropriate.

Glyph anatomy and the font production gate are documented in `packages/assets/fonts/MOONWITNESS-SIGNAL.md`. No third-party font binaries are used by the product. Do not call the proprietary font production-ready until the full Latin/Indonesian glyph set, metrics, kerning, WOFF2 build, and rendering tests are complete.

## Texture, accessibility, motion
- Fine orbital grids, lunar contours, restrained halftone, paper grain, archival rules.
- Texture stays low-opacity and never sits behind dense data or essential text.
- Meaningful illustrations require useful alt text; decorative assets must be hidden from assistive technology.
- Respect reduced motion. Never encode meaning through color or texture alone.
- Maintain WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, visible focus, keyboard operation, and readable zoom/reflow.

## Forbidden shortcuts
Generic AI gradients, generic surveillance-eye clichés, fake coordinates, pseudo-scientific ornament, RIOTWORKS studio styling as the default, copied marks, unlicensed assets, and sacred symbols used as decoration.
