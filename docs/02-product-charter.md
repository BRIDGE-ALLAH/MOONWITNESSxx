# 02 — MOONWITNESS Product Charter

**Status:** Approved baseline — aligned to MOONWITNESS master brand
**Version:** 1.1
**Updated:** 2026-11-08

## 1. Product definition

MOONWITNESS is an evidence-first knowledge and operations ecosystem. Its initial product experience helps a solo operator or small creator team discover evidence-backed opportunities, create original digital works, govern assets and provenance, prepare compliant distribution, account for revenue and costs, and learn from portfolio outcomes.

These creative-commerce workflows are one applied domain of the wider MOONWITNESS promise: SEE MORE. KNOW MORE. The human purpose is expressed by the Brand Manifesto: WHEN THE MOON RISES, WE SHARE MESSAGES.

RIOTWORKS is the product and operating division within MOONWITNESS, initially owning the creative-commerce workflow. It is not a replacement for the MOONWITNESS master brand. The wider product catalog and application boundaries are documented in [docs/32](./32-universe-and-product-architecture-registry.md).

The defining objective is to make repeatable operations increasingly autonomous while keeping the owner accountable for product judgment, rights, policy-sensitive actions, finance, and exceptions.

## 2. Primary user

Initial user: one owner/operator based in Indonesia who wants to sell original digital products and media to Indonesian and global buyers without running a client-service or outbound-sales business.

The system must support:
- Indonesian operator setup and local payout checks.
- English-first global listing preparation with Indonesian UI.
- Local currency and foreign-currency accounting.
- Hybrid local/cloud AI.
- Desktop-first operation with mobile monitoring.

## 3. User jobs

1. Find market opportunities without manually browsing every marketplace all day.
2. Understand the buyer problem and verify it with evidence.
3. Decide whether a product is worth making.
4. Create a genuinely differentiated product and its supporting assets.
5. Check IP, licenses, technical requirements, listing accuracy, and platform rules.
6. Prepare distribution through permitted channels.
7. Track revenue, fees, refunds, payout status, and costs.
8. Learn which products deserve improvement, expansion, or retirement.
9. Reduce repetitive operations and keep a clear audit trail.

## 4. Product principles

- **Marketplace-first discovery:** prioritize existing buyer-search and recommendation ecosystems.
- **Demand before production:** do not create at scale without a reason supported by evidence.
- **Original utility:** every product must have a clear buyer, job, and differentiator.
- **Proof over hype:** separate observed facts, estimates, assumptions, and generated suggestions.
- **Quality gates:** rights and policy failures block distribution.
- **Portfolio compounding:** products have lifecycle, versions, and performance histories.
- **Channel independence:** own source files and metadata; do not depend on one marketplace.
- **Safe autonomy:** use official APIs and permitted integrations only.
- **Human accountability:** approval is required for material rights, policy, money, and irreversible actions.
- **Cost awareness:** compute, storage, listing, payment, and promotion fees are part of the product economics.

## 5. What the MOONWITNESS creative-commerce experience is

- A market-intelligence workspace.
- A product and asset production coordinator.
- A provenance and quality-control system.
- A marketplace adapter and listing-preparation hub.
- A portfolio and unit-economics ledger.
- An agent and workflow operations console.

## 6. What it is not

- A guarantee of passive income.
- A bot that publishes unlimited AI output.
- A way to bypass marketplace rules, moderation, or account limits.
- A customer acquisition or cold-outreach CRM.
- A dropshipping or inventory fulfillment platform.
- A replacement for accounting, tax, legal, or professional review.
- A marketplace itself in the initial release.
- A system that fabricates demand, reviews, traffic, sales, or conversion data.

## 7. Meaning of "no customer hunting"

The operating strategy avoids cold outreach, manual prospect lists, and direct sales calls. It prioritizes marketplaces and platforms where buyers already search, browse, or watch.

This does not remove the need for discovery optimization, listing quality, honest content, customer support, refunds, platform moderation, or product updates. Marketplace traffic is not guaranteed and some marketplaces require initial genuine sales before internal discovery features become available.

## 8. Release boundary

### Prototype
Frontend-only, synthetic data, simulated interactions, no real account connections.

### MVP
One end-to-end product family, evidence capture, provenance, inspection, one permitted distribution path, and reliable revenue-cost records.

### Growth
More product factories, marketplaces, localization, scheduled intelligence, and deeper automation.

### Autonomy
Only after each channel's official integration, policies, and failure behavior are validated.

## 9. Success definition

MOONWITNESS succeeds when it helps people and operators build a repeatable, lawful, quality-controlled process where:
- Product decisions are supported by evidence.
- Production cost is measurable.
- Listings accurately represent delivered products.
- Distribution actions are auditable.
- Net contribution can be reconciled.
- Knowledge and provenance can be shared responsibly.
- The portfolio becomes more useful and resilient over time.

Income is an outcome to measure, not a feature to promise.
