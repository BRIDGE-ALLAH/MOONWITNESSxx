# Divine rights, privacy and preservation governance

## Ownership boundary
MoonWitness Corpus remains the canonical owner of source artifacts, rights statements, attribution, source-local identifiers, external identifiers and provenance. Divine consumes those records through read-only ports. A Divine projection is derived data, not a new rights grant or canonical record.

## Rights are not evidence quality
A source can be authentic but restricted; public but not licensed for redistribution; licensed but methodologically weak; or metadata-visible while its payload is unavailable. Rights, availability, provenance, evidential relevance and assessment are independent dimensions. Never collapse them into one confidence or trust score.

## Required states
- `cleared`: a specific license/permission and scope are recorded;
- `restricted`: access or redistribution is prohibited or limited;
- `metadata-only`: descriptive metadata may be shown, payload may not;
- `unknown`: rights are not established; do not redistribute;
- `withdrawn`: a prior grant or publication state was withdrawn;
- `expired`: a time-bounded permission has ended.

Unknown rights are not permission. Public GitHub visibility is not a license.

## Minimum rights record
Preserve rights holder/authority, exact license identifier or permission reference, source URI, attribution text, permitted uses, redistribution status, derivative-work restrictions, territory, time bounds, access conditions and evidence for the rights assertion. Keep the original source wording and provenance; do not normalize away restrictions.

## AI and derived outputs
AI extraction, translation, transcription, embeddings, summaries and annotations do not automatically acquire broader rights than their source. Derived output must retain source and rights lineage. Restricted payloads must not be copied into prompts, model datasets, caches or public artifacts unless the recorded permission explicitly allows it.

## Import gate for public repositories
Before importing Corpus data into public MOONWITNESS, review every dataset/artifact. Include only material with explicit redistribution permission. For unclear or restricted payloads, retain only metadata that is itself lawful to redistribute, plus source reference and a documented exclusion reason. Do not generate substitute text to fill a gap.

## Preservation and deletion
Prefer archiving over deletion. Do not close/delete an upstream repository until the approved destination has been verified by file inventory, content hashes, rights review, clean-checkout validation and an independently recoverable backup. MIZAN assessment does not replace legal rights review.
