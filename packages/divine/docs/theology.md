# Revelation and Theology Research Profiles

A revelation-related statement is represented as an attributed, scoped research claim. It is not a metaphysical verification.

Claim kinds distinguish theological attribution, canonical recognition, textual attestation, historical-critical assessment and editorial status. Every claim references a canonical Corpus assertion, source, evidence and provenance record, plus an explicit scope and method. Textual witness identity and transmission remain Corpus concerns.

Positions preserve affirm/deny/contest/undetermined stances, sources, period and scope. Comparison frames declare dimensions and methods. Outputs can explicitly be incomparable or non-equivalent; a comparison never implies a universal ranking.

Domain review records the reviewer, role, target, decision, rationale and authority scope. A reviewer is not presumed to speak for every adherent or every community.
